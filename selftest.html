Self test

Runs every PBQ's reference answer through the real graders, the same code path the app uses, and checks it scores full marks. A PBQ that fails here has requirements that cannot all be satisfied at once. Also exercises the misconception loop end to end and the scheduler under a seeded random, so a failing queue reproduces.

Feel: the first 100 ms

The four controls touched most, measured 2026-10-13 by the latency scenario with the CPU slowed fourfold, from the input to the frame that shows the result; a press is timed with a real mouse press held down, from the press event to the first frame that paints the outline. Every acknowledgement is a border, an outline or a glyph; motion is 120 ms and off under reduce-motion; nothing moves but the thing acted on. check_materials.py fails on any transition at or over 150 ms.
